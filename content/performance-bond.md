**Performance Bond** is a bond between a bidder and customer that protects either parties post an a contract has been finalized. It protects the parties ensuring things go as per the contract.

For example, if the work done by a bidder is poor or defective, the customer can make a claim against the performance bond. The bond provides compensation for the cost of redoing or correcting the job.

Likewise, if the customer fails to meet payment schedules post raising of invoices, the bidder can make a claim against the performance bond.
