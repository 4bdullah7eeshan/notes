**Internal Acceptance Test (IAT)** is a test for internal staff or a dedicated internal team to test a system or application to identify and rectify any internal issues before it's presented to external users.

The goal is to ensure the system works correctly from a technical standpoint and is ready for the next phase, which would be [[user-acceptance-test\|UAT]].
