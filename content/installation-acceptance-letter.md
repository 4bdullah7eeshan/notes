**Installation Acceptance Letter** is a formal document from a client or customer to a supplier or contractor, officially acknowledging that an installed system or equipment meets the agreed-upon specifications and is functioning correctly.

This letter serves to finalize the installation process, confirms that the customer accepts the installation, and marks the transfer of responsibility from the supplier to the customer, often triggering the start of warranty periods or final payments.
