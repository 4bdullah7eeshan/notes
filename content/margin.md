**Margin (or profit margin)** represents the percentage of revenue that remains after accounting for the cost of goods sold.

It is expressed as a percentage of the **selling price**.

## Formula

$$ \text{Margin (currency)} = \text{Selling Price} - \text{Cost Price} $$
$$ \text{Margin Percentage} = \left( \frac{\text{Selling Price} - \text{Cost Price}}{\text{Selling Price}} \right) \times 100\% $$
## Example

- Cost Price = $80$
- Selling Price = $100$
- Margin = $100 - 80 = 20$
- Margin Percentage = $\left( \frac{100 - 80}{100} \right) \times 100\% = 20\%$
