**Markup** is the amount added to the cost price of goods to cover overhead and profit.

It is calculated as a percentage of the **cost price**.

## Formula

$$ \text{Markup (currency)} = \text{Selling Price} - \text{Cost Price} $$
$$ \text{Markup Percentage} = \left( \frac{\text{Selling Price} - \text{Cost Price}}{\text{Cost Price}} \right) \times 100\% $$

## Example

- Cost Price = $80$
- Selling Price = $100$
- Markup = $100 - 80$ = $20$
- Markup Percentage = $\left( \frac{100 - 80}{80} \right) \times 100\% = 25\%$
